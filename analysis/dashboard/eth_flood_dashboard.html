≋Shabelle & Genale Flood Watch

Will the rivers of Ethiopia's Somali region flood in the coming Deyr rains (October–December 2026)? This page checks the latest 10-day GloFAS river forecast every day and raises a flag when flood levels become likely.

How to read this page
  1. The forecast is a bundle of 51 runs. GloFAS runs the same 10-day river forecast 51 times with slightly different starting weather. In each station panel, the shaded band is the range those runs cover (dark: the middle half; light: the widest 80%), and the solid line is the middle run.
  2. The dashed grey lines are that river's Deyr flood levels, estimated from 46 years of model history: moderate is reached about once every 2 years, high about once every 3, severe about once every 5. Bigger floods are rarer.
  3. A signal means runs are crossing a flood level. The small bars under each panel show, day by day, what share of the 51 runs are above the high level. When at least half the runs cross a level on a day inside October–December, the station changes color and the matching monitored condition in the overview is marked reached.
  4. Before October the page stays quiet on purpose. The rivers naturally run high in July–September (the Ethiopian highlands' Kiremt rains), so days outside the Deyr window are shown muted and never activate a trigger.
