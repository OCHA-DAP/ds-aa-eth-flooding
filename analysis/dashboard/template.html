Ethiopia Riverine Flood Watch

Daily monitoring of GloFAS river forecasts on the seven river systems the EDRMC lists as riverine flood risks: Wabi Shebelle, Genale Dawa, lower Omo, Bilate, the Abaya-Chamo lakes, Baro and Akobo. The trigger is reached when any river system is forecast at or over its OND threshold on a forecast day between 1 October and 31 December.

How to read this page

  • GloFAS runs the same 10-day forecast 51 times with slightly different starting weather; this page uses the ensemble median for each day. Only forecast days falling in October to December count.
  • Each river's threshold starts at the peak of its 3rd-largest OND season on the 2003 to 2025 GloFAS record and is lowered where that catches further seasons already inside the trigger's activation years, without letting any new year in: crossing it is a 1-in-8 to 1-in-6 year event depending on the river. The depth was chosen so that the trigger as a whole (any river over its threshold) has an overall activation frequency of 1-in-3 years: on the record it activates in 8 of 23 years (2008, 2011, 2013, 2017, 2019, 2023, 2024, 2025).
  • The banner, the shaded zones on the map, the river charts and the table all show this one rule. Zone shading follows the river system that puts the zone at risk.
