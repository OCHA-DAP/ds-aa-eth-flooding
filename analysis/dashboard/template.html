How to read this page

  • GloFAS runs the same 10-day forecast 51 times with slightly different starting weather; this page uses the ensemble median for each day.
  • The 1-in-3 year level is seasonal, fitted per station on 2003 to 2025 GloFAS reanalysis (Gu March to June, July to September highland flows, Deyr October to December). Each forecast day is compared with its own season's level, because these rivers naturally run much higher in July to September, fed by rain in the Ethiopian highlands upstream, than during the local Gu or Deyr rains.
  • A station is marked reached when the median is at or above the level on any of the 10 days. The map, the chart and the table all show this one check.
